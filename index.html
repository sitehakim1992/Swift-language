🛡️ Our Privacy Commitment

SwiftLearn does NOT directly collect, store, or transmit any personal identification data on our servers.

All your learning progress, quiz results, and settings are stored locally on your device. To keep this educational application free, we integrate third-party mobile advertising services.

1. Information Collection

Direct Collection by Developer: NONE

We do not directly collect or ask for personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Third-Party Data Collection (Advertising & Analytics):

To support app maintenance, SwiftLearn uses third-party advertising services (Start.io). These services may automatically collect certain technical, non-personally identifiable information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID/OAID) and unique device parameters.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, and mobile network info.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic region derived from IP address.
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, clicks, and interactions.

2. Data Storage

All data related to your learning progress is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage). This includes:

  • Lesson completion status
  • Quiz scores and results
  • Learning streaks and XP points
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
  • Challenge completion status

This learning data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising Services

We display third-party advertisements provided by Start.io Inc. to sustain our free learning platform.

These ad networks may collect technical identifiers to deliver relevant advertisements and prevent fraud. You can review how Start.io manages user data by reading their official policy:

👉 Start.io Privacy Policy

Opting Out: You can reset or restrict personalized ad tracking directly from your Android device settings via Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for general audiences interested in learning programming. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal data has been transmitted through third-party ad networks, please contact us for immediate assistance.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy in the future, changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes implies acceptance of the updated terms.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

Since your learning progress is stored locally, you have total control over it:

  • You can reset all learning progress within the app's Statistics screen.
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all locally stored progress data from your device.

7. Security

We take security seriously. Your local data is protected by your device's built-in security features (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, Face ID, etc.).
